Question

What is the primary function of the Mitochondria?

Answer
volume_up

The Powerhouse of the Cell

Mitochondria generate most of the chemical energy needed to power the cell's biochemical reactions.

auto_awesome AI Insight

Chemical energy produced by the mitochondria is stored in a small molecule called adenosine triphosphate (ATP). While they are present in nearly all types of human cells, they are vital for survival.

In addition to producing energy, mitochondria store calcium for cell signaling activities, generate heat, and mediate cell growth and death.
